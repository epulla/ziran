# Ziran brand notes

## Character

A quiet, observant, nimble fox. Orange fur, cream muzzle and tail tip, dark paws and nose. Friendly rather than hyperactive. The approved full illustration remains the character reference; the fox-head trace is its compact brand mark.

## Palette

| Token | Hex |
|---|---|
| Fox orange | `#FF7A20` |
| Sunset | `#FFB45B` |
| Cream | `#FFF4E5` |
| Warm gray | `#E5D8CA` |
| Earth | `#826450` |
| Charcoal | `#20272F` |

These are selected working colors, not a claim that every shaded pixel in the concept board uses an exact swatch. Orange is an accent, not automatically an accessible text color; check contrast for actual interface use.

## Identity

Use lowercase `ziran` in the wordmark and `Ziran` in prose and system app metadata. The proposed line is “Your windows. In flow.” Keep a little clear space around the symbol and wordmark. Do not stretch the artwork or recolor the fox blue/yellow.

Use the illustrated seated fox for hero placements and the app icon. Use the compact head in the menu bar and tiny placements. Keep the original warm background behind the small pose panels; use the separate transparent hero where a clean silhouette is needed.

The menu-bar symbol is black and clear and must be rendered as an AppKit template. Do not manually switch between a baked black and white background image for light/dark modes.

## Limitations

The artwork originated in the approved AI-generated concept board. No trademark clearance or exclusivity review is implied. No font files, Apple artwork or third-party mascot assets are included. The wordmark files contain paths, not a bundled typeface.
